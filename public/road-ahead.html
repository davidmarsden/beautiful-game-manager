The Road Ahead

This page explains the constitutional destination beyond the current controlled alpha. It is not a promise that every system will arrive in a particular order or release.

Read current Alpha Rules Alpha Guide Manager Portal

The idea

TBG is intended to simulate a football world, not just football matches: clubs, players and managers with histories, incentives and consequences that persist for many seasons.

Football knowledge and judgement should matter more than clicking fastest, exploiting hidden formulas or inheriting the richest owner.

01

Players make career decisions

Players will weigh wages, security, playing time, club strength, reputation, trophies, development, manager reputation, ambition, career stage and geography. The highest wage or biggest club will not automatically win every recruitment battle.

02

Recruitment Windows

When several clubs compete for an available player, Recruitment Windows are intended to give managers a fair chance to make their case before the player chooses — replacing first-click races with judgement.

03

Agents, contracts and promises

Contracts can become richer through wages, length, bonuses, clauses, playing status, development pathways and ambitions. Managers may make promises; keeping them should build reputation, breaking them should matter.

04

Scouting as knowledge

Every manager should ultimately be able to find the same real footballers. The competitive edge is how well you understand them. Managers will build expertise in limited Knowledge Regions, with accumulated knowledge carrying value and changing focus carrying a cost.

05

Young players and development

Clubs that genuinely identify, play and develop young players should build credibility. Development history can affect future attraction and may generate Development Levy compensation when qualifying players move.

06

Clubs with expectations

Each job will eventually have an Expected Finish and a public Contract Objective: avoid relegation, consolidate, top half, promotion, title challenge or a specified trophy. Success is judged relative to expectation, not league position alone.

07

Board Confidence

Managers will not have jobs for life. Persistent underperformance can put a manager at risk through a published, deterministic Board Confidence system rather than administrator whim.

08

Earned Board Backing

Managers who outperform expectations can earn additional Board Backing. The aim is to reward excellent management without introducing arbitrary rich owners.

09

A deeper football economy

The alpha starts with cash and wage affordability. The wider economy adds revenue, expected-wage curves, scouting investment, transfer budgets, market-value controls, sustainability rules and deterministic sanctions such as transfer restrictions, wage restrictions and points deductions.

10

Transfer rumours and information

Not every transfer action should instantly be public. Serious or repeated pursuit can generate rumours without exposing sealed bid details, creating a strategic information layer around recruitment.

11

A living football press

World Feed v0 is only the beginning. The destination includes match reports, league roundups, transfer stories, records, milestones and pre/post-match questions. Managers should normally make meaningful choices rather than write essays.

12

Manager reputation and identity

Careers can reflect trophies, promotions, performance against expectations, youth development, financial stewardship, promises kept, tenure and stability. Styles should emerge from behaviour: you do not select Youth Developer or Promotion Specialist; you become one.

13

Professionalism and participation

TBG should reward reliable participation without demanding constant presence. The design includes Professionalism, reasonable response times, Away Mode and a staged abandonment process: Warning → Caretaker → Removal.

14

Promotion playoffs

The canonical league structure uses three automatic promotion places, a four-team 4th–7th playoff for the fourth place, and four automatic relegation places. This is already governance, but the live engine still needs the playoff implementation.

15

Cups and a broader calendar

The wider world includes domestic cups and leaves room for future continental or international competitions, giving managers more ways to create history than league position alone.

16

Loans and complex deals

The transaction architecture is intended to expand into first-class loans, automatic returns, multi-player exchanges and three-or-more-club transactions — without compromising the rule that a deal either completes correctly as a whole or leaves the canonical world unchanged.

17

What will not change

  • one persistent shared world
  • public rules
  • real-world evidence as the basis of player quality
  • in-world ownership independent of real-world transfers
  • no secret admin player boosts
  • no click-race advantage for important recruitment decisions
  • no unequal sugar-daddy owners
  • no direct press-conference bonus to match results
  • no rewriting Ability because of one TBG performance
  • no sacrificing transaction safety or world integrity for feature breadth

The destination

The long-term TBG manager should have to think about much more than the next formation: who fits the squad, who will sign, which markets you understand, which young players deserve minutes, which promises you can keep, what the club can afford, how patient the board is — and what sort of manager you are becoming.

Eventually, the question is simple: what will the history of this one shared football world say you achieved?
